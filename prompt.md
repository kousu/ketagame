> Done. The game compiles successfully and contains 63 decision nodes across 3 scenes, well above the 30-node minimum.
>
> To play: ./ketagame (or go run main.go)
>
> Game Structure
>
> Scene 1: The Arrival - The player arrives at SpaceZ and meets Melon Husk in various states (floating in a sensory deprivation tank, paranoid on a platform, or gazing out a window). Multiple entry points based on how you approach his door lead to branching philosophical conversations.
>
> Scene 2: The Ketamine Philosophy Session - A deep philosophical debate about whether altered consciousness can produce valid ideas about humanity's future. The player can argue:
>
> - Deontologically (impaired judgment is always wrong)
> - Consequentially (results matter most)
> - With nuance (depends on the type of alteration)
> - Epistemologically (what even is "normal" consciousness?)
>
> Each position gets challenged by Melon's counterarguments.
>
> Scene 3: The Vision and Final Reckoning - Melon shows his Mars vision and asks for the player's honest assessment. The player's final choices determine one of five endings:
>
> - The Balanced Path (good) - Becoming an accountability voice
> - The Observer (neutral) - Walking away with unanswered questions
> - The Socratic Way (philosophical) - Finding value in questions over answers
> - The Unexamined Life (bad) - Being seduced by the visionary's logic
> - Beyond the Veil (transcendent) - Glimpsing something ineffable
>
> The central philosophical theme throughout: can ideas for humanity's future be trusted when they emerge from chemically-altered states, or does the origin of an idea matter less than its merit?
